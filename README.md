# Wendy — Foundation Model Systems & Research Engineering

Live portfolio: **https://wendy-james.github.io/**

A warm editorial, mobile-friendly research portfolio featuring **publicly verifiable** work in foundation-model systems, post-training × agentic RL research questions, and offline ML evaluation. Personal education details and personal contact addresses are intentionally not published.

## Highlighted work

- **[WendyFM](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model)** — completed byte-level BPE tokenizer milestone, [technical report](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model/blob/main/report/tokenizer.md) and [benchmark CSV](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model/blob/main/benchmarks/tokenizer/results.csv).
- **Post-training and RL trainability** — private research in progress, with no completed RL/publication claims.
- **Applied ML** — [recommendation retrieval](https://github.com/Wendy-James/short-video-recsys-reproduce), [multimodal retrieval](https://github.com/Wendy-James/ecommerce-multimodal-retrieval), and [RAG evaluation](https://github.com/Wendy-James/llm-rag-system), all public offline evaluation/reproduction projects.

The tokenizer speedups are small **synthetic CPU tests**, including a negative Chinese result and memory overhead. The page does not claim online business results, production deployment, or completed large-scale training.
