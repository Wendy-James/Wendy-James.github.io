# Wendy — LLM Post-training × Agentic RL

Live portfolio: **https://wendy-james.github.io/**

A mobile-friendly research portfolio with **Post-training × Agentic RL as the primary research agenda** and reproducible systems experiments as supporting evidence. The public site intentionally omits legal identity, education details and personal contact addresses.

## Highlighted work

- **Post-training × Agentic RL** — primary ongoing research direction, focused on verifier reliability, SFT-to-RL experiment design, tool-use feedback and trajectory evaluation. Research remains private; no completed RL, publication or agent performance claims.
- **[WendyFM](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model)** — completed byte-level BPE tokenizer milestone, [technical report](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model/blob/main/report/tokenizer.md), [benchmark CSV](https://github.com/Wendy-James/wendy-foundation-model/blob/main/benchmarks/tokenizer/results.csv), and an unmerged Transformer draft.
- **Applied ML** — [recommendation retrieval](https://github.com/Wendy-James/short-video-recsys-reproduce), [multimodal retrieval](https://github.com/Wendy-James/ecommerce-multimodal-retrieval), and [RAG evaluation](https://github.com/Wendy-James/llm-rag-system), all public offline evaluation/reproduction projects.

The tokenizer speedups are small **synthetic CPU tests**, including a negative Chinese result and memory overhead. The page does not claim online business results, production deployment, or completed large-scale training.

## Structure

- `index.html`: semantic static HTML, selected work and direct evidence links
- `styles.css`: responsive editorial visual system (cream, warm terracotta, serif/sans typography)

GitHub Pages deploys from the configured branch. The homepage uses a neutral typographic monogram instead of a personally identifying portrait.

Publication policy: never put employer-owned code, internal metrics, datasets, credentials, or unreleased private research in the public site.

The source-of-truth editorial checklist is [PORTFOLIO_MAINTENANCE.md](https://github.com/Wendy-James/Wendy-James/blob/main/PORTFOLIO_MAINTENANCE.md). The site must not turn future research directions into claims of shipped results.
